Privacy notice

This notice describes data processing in the Android game QuaTD: Sci-Fi Tower Defense and on this information website. The game is offered worldwide and is not specifically directed at children.

The game itself does not require a separate QuaTD account. Game saves are stored locally. Optional Google services, the optional “Ad-free” purchase through Google Play and externally opened support services may process data outside your device.

2. Local saves and backups

The app uses shared_preferences to store data in local app storage. This includes:

  • highscores, experience points and diamonds;
  • mission stars, unlocks, talents, research and achievements;
  • tutorial progress and language, audio and display settings;
  • the status of the “Ad-free” option (unlocked, source of the unlock and time);
  • dates and times, daily advertising limits and reward receipts that prevent duplicate credits.

These data support saved progress, settings and correct reward allocation. The audited app has no operator-run server receiving the complete game save. Transmission of Endless scores to Google Play Games is described in section 5.

Android/Google backups: Depending on your device, operating system and backup settings, Android or Google may back up app data or transfer it to another device. The app does not expressly disable these backups. We therefore cannot assure you that local data will always remain exclusively on the original device. You can manage backup settings through your device or Google account.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

QuaTD uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded ads. These may be offered for diamonds, talent respec, a Daily Case bonus or an additional reward after a Highscore Run. Watching an ad is not required for the basic game. The audited integration contains no banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

In the published game, Google serves real rewarded ads. Test and pre-release versions use Google test ads; this does not mean that the advertising SDK processes no data. While the “Ad-free” option (section 4) is active, the app requests no ads.

According to Google, the Mobile Ads SDK may in particular process:

  • IP addresses, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • interactions with the app and advertising, such as views, clicks and video views;
  • technical diagnostic and performance data;
  • advertising IDs, app set IDs and, where applicable, other device- or account-related identifiers.

Google identifies advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as purposes. Processing in an individual case depends in part on SDK, account and privacy settings. In the audited ad request, the app does not supply a separate QuaTD account ID or additional personal audience attributes of its own.

Preloading: Ads may be requested when you open a related screen. Data processing therefore does not necessarily begin only when you tap “Watch ad”.

For live ads, the app uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): it requests consent information, displays the configured form when required and then checks whether ads may be requested. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form with the choices “Consent”, “Do not consent” and “Manage options” appears before the first ad. If you do not consent, Google may serve limited, non-personalised ads under its own policies. The form is only requested when a rewarded ad is requested. Explicit test ads use a separate flow without this UMP form. The audited ad request does not enforce a general “non-personalised ads only” setting.

When UMP requires additional privacy options, the app provides access in the relevant reward screens. You can reopen the available choices there. You can also manage the advertising ID through the applicable Android settings.

Further information: Google Privacy Policy, Mobile Ads SDK data processing and Google UMP.

4. “Ad-free” purchase through Google Play

You can optionally buy the “Ad-free” option as a one-time in-app purchase. Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details such as card or account information are not entered in QuaTD, and the app does not receive them. Google processes the data needed for the purchase under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play terms.

The app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play, including a purchase identifier, and acknowledges the purchase to Google. Locally it stores only that the option is unlocked, where the unlock came from and when it was saved. After a reinstall or on a new device, the app asks Google Play whether the purchase belongs to your Google account and restores the option. QuaTD does not run its own server for purchases.

While the option is active, the app requests no ads and does not call up an advertising consent form. Rewards are credited directly; the daily limits stay the same. The Google Mobile Ads SDK remains part of the app. Test versions may use a clearly labelled simulated test purchase that charges nothing and has no connection to Google Play Billing.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and Google Play — purchases and refunds.

5. Google Play Games

On Android, Google Play Games Services v2 is integrated for sign-in and the Endless leaderboard. The SDK is initialised when the app starts, if available and configured. The app checks authentication status and offers sign-in through the leaderboard feature. Google sign-in is not required for normal gameplay.

The app uses the Play Games player ID for association and to detect an account change. After a valid completed Endless run, its final score is submitted to Google if connected. Completing missions or merely loading a saved local highscore does not trigger this submission. The leaderboard opens in Google's own interface.

QuaTD has no separate password-based account system. The audited flow does not read Google passwords, email addresses, friend lists or Google cloud game saves. Google additionally processes its own profile, analytics and diagnostic data for Play Games. Visibility of your profile and game information depends on your Google profile settings.

Deleting local progress does not remove leaderboard data already stored by Google. You can separately manage or delete your Play Games data through your Play Games profile or Google account. Further information: Google Play Games — data processing and deletion.

6. Voluntary support through PayPal

If you select the PayPal support link, the app opens PayPal through an external handler, such as your browser. There is no PayPal SDK, no payment processing within QuaTD and no in-game benefit in return. The app does not receive or store payment status or payment details through this flow.

When you visit PayPal and make a voluntary payment, PayPal processes data under its own terms. Outside the app, the recipient may receive information about the payment and the payer. The specific information depends on the PayPal transaction; this notice therefore does not claim that payments are anonymous. See the PayPal Privacy Statement.

7. Email support

After you choose the contact button, it opens a draft in an email application addressed to acidsstudio@gmail.com, with the subject “QuaTD Feedback”. QuaTD does not send this message itself and, in the audited flow, does not automatically attach game saves or diagnostic reports.

If you send the message, your sender address, message content and information you attach are processed by the email services involved and the recipient to handle your enquiry. You decide what further information to provide.

8. Permissions and technical data

The Android app has internet/network permissions and SDK permissions for advertising identifiers and Android advertising services, plus the Google Play Billing permission for the “Ad-free” purchase. The audited release manifest contained no access permissions for precise device location, contacts, camera or microphone. Approximate location may nevertheless be inferred from an IP address.

The app writes technical status and error messages, for example about ads, Google authentication and score submission, to local diagnostic output. No operator-run remote log upload or analytics backend was identified. Firebase, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not present in the audited integration. This does not exclude diagnostic or analytics processing by the Google SDKs themselves.

Fonts and game media are distributed with the app. Automatic downloading of Google Fonts is disabled. The version display reads the running app's version and build number; it does not call a separate web service for this purpose.

9. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains stored for later play sessions. “Reset Progress” deletes the designated progress data; it does not remove all language, audio and other settings or external account connections. Android also provides functions to clear app data or uninstall the app. Existing backups and data held by external services may remain separately. The “Ad-free” purchase stays linked to your Google account; a local reset does not remove it, and Google Play continues to record it.

Data independently processed by Google, PayPal or GitHub is subject to their respective retention and deletion procedures. The app cannot erase all such data through a local reset.

Outstanding information: Fixed retention periods for support correspondence and any PayPal records accessible to the operator have not yet been confirmed for this notice. Specific retention periods of external providers have also not been verified here. To request information or deletion concerning data held by the operator, please use the privacy contact above.

The particular entities, processing locations and any transfer mechanisms cannot be fully determined from the app. Further relevant information is available in the providers' linked privacy notices. This notice does not give a verified assurance of particular safeguards, certifications or contractual mechanisms.

12. Children

QuaTD is not specifically directed at children. This statement does not establish a particular age rating or age-group selection in Google Play. That classification is determined separately according to the actual game content. Parents or guardians with questions about data processing can use the privacy contact.

13. This website

This privacy notice website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser retrieves its HTML and CSS files. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics, JavaScript tracking or externally loaded fonts. Language switching uses ordinary links and is not stored in a cookie.

GitHub states that it logs and stores IP addresses of GitHub Pages visitors for security purposes, including visitors who are not signed in to GitHub. This hosting processing is separate from the optional services within the app. Details: GitHub Pages — data collection and GitHub Privacy Statement.

External information links are opened only when you click them. The destination website is then subject to its own privacy notice.
